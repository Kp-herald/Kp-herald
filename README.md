## About

I am an aspiring **Data Analyst & Business Analyst** with a Computer Science engineering background and practical experience building data-driven solutions using **Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and Statistics**.

My work focuses on converting raw datasets into structured information, actionable insights, and decision-ready dashboards. I have hands-on exposure to **EDA, ETL concepts, data cleaning, feature engineering, data modeling, DAX, SQL analytics, and business intelligence**.

I approach analytics with an engineering and product mindset — understand the problem, work with reliable data, identify meaningful patterns, and communicate insights through clear and interactive analytical products.
